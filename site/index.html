FAQ

Before you download.

Does Lizard '13 include FIFA 13?

No. You need your own legitimate PC installation. The launcher does not distribute FIFA 13, game executables or EA-owned game files.

Is this connected to Electronic Arts?

No. Lizard '13 is an independent community preservation/revival project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Electronic Arts.

Why is access invite-only?

The service is still being hardened and tested. A controlled beta lets the project validate capacity, economy behaviour and remaining client flows without pretending it is already a finished public service.

Will my club disappear when the launcher updates?

No. Club state is server-side. Updating or replacing the launcher does not reset your FUT progress.

Where do I get support?

Discord support is coming soon. Never post passwords, recovery codes, device secrets or authentication material in public channels or screenshots.
